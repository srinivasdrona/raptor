# R611Q structural-rescue feasibility brief for laboratory discussion

> **Purpose:** request feasibility feedback and define experiments. This is not a compound
> recommendation, treatment proposal, or claim of demonstrated rescue.

## Question

Can a reproducible structural intervention site for $TSC2$ $p.\mathrm{Arg611Gln}$ be identified
using experimental structures and open computational tools, and can hypotheses at that site be
falsified with practical protein, complex, and functional assays?

## Evidence motivating the work

The literature supports, in engineered systems:

- reduced $\mathrm{TSC1}$ binding or complex-associated signal;
- reduced steady-state R611Q $\mathrm{TSC2}$ abundance;
- Pam/MYCBP2-associated ubiquitination despite $\mathrm{TSC1}$ co-expression;
- impaired TSC-complex-dependent $\mathrm{mTORC1}$ suppression.

Wild-type TSC-complex structures PDB `7DL2` and `9CE3` contain residue $611$. No R611Q structure,
validated ligandable site, compound binder, or rescue experiment is known in the synthesized
corpus.

## Candidate rescue mechanisms to test

1. **Local stabilization:** binding to a reproducible local or nearby allosteric site increases
   stable R611Q abundance.
2. **Interface stabilization:** binding at or coupled to the $\mathrm{TSC1}$-$\mathrm{TSC2}$
   interface increases complex formation.
3. **Distal allostery:** binding elsewhere favors a complex-competent, GAP-competent state.
4. **Null outcome:** no structurally or experimentally tractable small-molecule rescue route exists.

## Questions for a laboratory collaborator

1. Which full-length, domain, or complex constructs can be expressed reproducibly for wild type
   and R611Q?
2. Can abundance, half-life, ubiquitination, and proteasome dependence be measured in a matched
   system?
3. Is a native $\mathrm{TSC1}$-$\mathrm{TSC2}$ complex assay such as co-immunoprecipitation
   tractable?
4. Which two orthogonal physical principles can establish direct binding for the available
   construct?
5. Can wild-type, R611Q-reconstituted, and $\mathrm{TSC2}^{-/-}$ isogenic backgrounds be compared?
6. Is a Rheb-GTP or another GAP-proximal readout practical, with $p$-S6K/$p$-S6 as corroboration?
7. Which aggregation, interference, inactive-analog, dose-response, viability, replicate, and lot
   controls are available?

## Proposed experimental sequence

1. Establish construct and assay reproducibility.
2. Measure the untreated R611Q protein-state baseline.
3. For any later computational hypothesis, screen physical target engagement.
4. Confirm binding with an orthogonal method.
5. Test route-specific protein or complex rescue.
6. Test target dependence in $\mathrm{TSC2}^{-/-}$ cells.
7. Test GAP-proximal function and downstream pathway state.
8. Reject effects explained by aggregation, interference, reactivity, or toxicity.

## Explicit falsifiers

- no reproducible R611Q protein-state or complex defect in the selected system;
- no structurally reproducible intervention site;
- candidate engagement without protein-state or complex rescue;
- pathway changes persisting in $\mathrm{TSC2}^{-/-}$ cells;
- disruption of wild-type complex or GAP function;
- aggregation, assay interference, or cytotoxicity;
- inability to establish a tractable orthogonal assay pair.

## What would be shared later

If structural feasibility is positive, a later package may contain a small set of chemically
diverse **molecular hypotheses** with coordinates, provenance, uncertainty, route-specific assays,
controls, and falsifiers. It must not label them leads, binders, therapies, or treatment
candidates before the relevant experimental evidence exists.
